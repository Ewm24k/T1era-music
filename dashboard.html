Beta Disclaimer

Welcome to T1ERA Studio

T1ERA Music is not a standard streaming media player. Rather, it serves as an interactive catalog designed to help you find tracks you wish to transcribe into interactive piano sheets.

All audio previews are limited to 30 seconds. These streams are solely provided to help you recognize and confirm the identity of the track before loading it into the studio.

Because T1ERA is actively in its Beta phase, not all catalog tracks have specialized, database-trained AI piano models yet. If a trained model exists for your selected song, the engine will transcribe it with high precision. Otherwise, the platform will utilize a generalized pre-trained model which, while capable, may produce a less refined transcription.
